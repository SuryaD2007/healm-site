Healm

The doctor that knows you.

Healm is an iPhone health companion. It keeps your health file on your phone, reads your Apple Health data each morning, gives you one number for the day and a line that explains it, and answers health questions using what it knows about you.

Healm is not your doctor yet. It gives information, not diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call 911.

Support

Questions, problems, or a request about your data: hello@healm.app.

Most things you can do in the app: in Healm, open You, then Settings. Export everything gives you a zip of your file. Delete everything removes your account from Healm's server and everything from your phone.
