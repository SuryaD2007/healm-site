Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Your health file lives on your phone. Healm's server holds an account with no name, your daily counts, and your email only if you join the clinician waitlist. When you ask Healm something, what that one answer needs passes through to its AI model, with your permission, and Healm keeps none of it.

Healm is not your doctor yet. It gives information, not diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call 911.

On your phone

Everything you put into Healm is stored on your iPhone, inside the app, protected by iOS file encryption and by Healm's Face ID lock:

  • Your health file: birth date (or the age you gave), sex, height and weight, goals, habits, conditions, surgeries, family history, medications and supplements, and allergies, including whether you said you take none or have none.
  • Lab results you add, with a small thumbnail of each report.
  • Your daily readings from Apple Health, your daily number, and its note.
  • Today's plan and what you ticked or logged, the patterns Healm noticed, and your step goal.
  • Your conversations with Healm, and small thumbnails of photos you sent with them.
  • Your scans and label reads, with small thumbnails. Full photos are never saved.
  • If you track food: your food log, the foods you saved, your calorie and protein targets, the weights you type in, and the form you last picked for a food, such as raw or cooked.

None of this is synced to iCloud or copied to Healm's server. The home screen and lock screen widgets see only your number, the note, the day count, the names of any flagged metrics, and today's steps and step goal. If you pin your step goal to the lock screen, it shows today's steps, the goal, and your number.

On Healm's server

Healm's server holds only what it needs to run the service. None of it is health information.

What passes through the AI model

Healm's answers in Ask, scan and label results, lab readings, and the morning note are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Healm asks your permission before the first one, and you can turn it off in Settings, under AI answers. Without it, nothing is sent and the morning note comes from Healm's own wording.

With your permission, each answer sends only what it needs, once:

  • A question sends your question, recent messages in that conversation, a photo if you attached one, and a summary of your file, which can include conditions, medications (or that you have not said yet whether you take any, so the answer can mention what commonly interacts), lab values, Apple Health readings like sleep and heart rate, today's step count and step goal, if you track food, what you have eaten today (each food and its numbers) against your targets, the training days your targets are set for, the names of foods you saved or logged recently, and your weight trend, and the conditions outside you looked up on Today: UV, air quality, pollen, and today's forecast, never your location.
  • Numbers you type to add as they are in Log food, such as "add 150 calories and 2g protein", are logged on your phone and sent nowhere. A question typed there, such as "what should I eat for dinner", is answered like any other question, above.
  • Other words typed in Log food are sent, so Claude can sort them into foods to look up, numbers to add as they are, and a question, split the foods into amounts, and name a brand or restaurant. It does not see your targets, your log, or your weight. Logging a food from one of Healm's answers sends that food's name the same way. Healm's server looks each food up in fatsecret (brands and restaurants) and USDA FoodData Central; only a food neither knows takes Claude's estimate, marked Estimate on its row, for you to keep or change. The names and numbers of foods you saved go to Healm's server with it, so your own foods match first, and are not kept.
  • A scan or label read sends the photos and a summary of your file.
  • A question about a skin, hair, or body scan also sends what Healm found in that scan, and with the first question only, the small thumbnails of its photos kept on your phone.
  • A lab report sends the report image or PDF.
  • The morning note sends the day's changes, such as last night's sleep against your usual, and a short summary of your file.

It goes from your phone to Healm's server, which passes it to Anthropic and returns the answer. Healm's server does not store it and never writes it to a log. Anthropic uses it to write the answer, does not train its models on it, and deletes it on the schedule its commercial terms set (within 30 days at the time of writing, longer only if a request is flagged for a safety review).

Apple Health

  • Healm only reads from Apple Health. It never writes to it.
  • It reads sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, active energy, blood oxygen, breathing rate, VO2 max, weight, body fat, height, and workouts, once a day and when new data arrives, and today's steps, active energy, and latest heart rate while Today is open.
  • The readings are stored on your phone. They are never stored on Healm's server and never synced to iCloud.
  • They leave your phone only inside an answer you allowed, as described above.
  • They are never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with data brokers.
  • You can change what Healm reads at any time: open Healm in the iOS Settings app, then Health.

Motion and location

  • Motion and Fitness. While Today is open, Healm counts today's steps, distance, and flights from your iPhone's motion sensors. They are shown and then let go. Only today's step count leaves your phone, with a question you send, so the answer can use it.
  • Location. Only if you tap Show conditions outside on Today, Healm asks for your approximate location, while you use the app, never always and never precise. It rounds the place to about a kilometer and sends it to Apple Weather for the UV index and today's forecast, and through Healm's server to Open-Meteo for air quality and Google for pollen. Healm does not store your location on its server or write it to a log, and keeps only the last reading on your phone, without the place, so Ask can answer a weather question from it. You can turn location off for Healm at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Today's patterns, plan, streaks, and charts are worked out on your phone. Nothing about them leaves it unless you tap into Ask.

Photos and the camera

Scan and label photos are held in memory only until their reply arrives, then released. Only a small thumbnail is saved, on your phone. Healm never saves photos to your photo library.

A food barcode is read on your phone, and only its digits are sent, through Healm's server, to look the product up. A nutrition label is read on your phone with Apple's text recognition: the photo never leaves your phone and is not saved, and you check the numbers before the food is kept.

Food and weight

Food tracking is optional. Your food log, targets, and weights stay on your phone. Weights come from what you type and from Apple Health, including a scale that syncs to it. If you allow notifications, one line about the day's food can arrive in the evening, worked out and scheduled on your phone, only on a day you logged something; you can turn it off in Settings. If your file names an eating disorder, Healm turns calorie and weight tracking off.

If you tap Set a reminder on Today's bedtime suggestion, one reminder for that night is scheduled on your phone. Healm never sets one on its own.

Emergencies

Before any question is sent, Healm checks its words on your phone against a list of warning signs. If they suggest an emergency, Healm shows a Call 911 button at once. If they suggest thoughts of self-harm, Healm shows the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, to call or text. Healm never calls anyone for you, and no person watches conversations. A count of how often a warning sign matched is kept on your phone, without the words.

No tracking, no ads

Healm has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no data brokers. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not sell or rent anything about you.

Export and delete

In Settings, Export everything makes a zip of your whole file you can read and keep.

Delete everything removes your account from Healm's server first: the account, your daily counts, your waitlist entry, and any Apple ID link. If you signed in with Apple, Healm also asks Apple to revoke its sign-in. Only when the server confirms does Healm erase everything on your phone. If the server cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and Healm tells you so. Apple Health keeps its own data, and an export you saved stays where you saved it.

Age

Healm is for people 18 and over. It is not meant for children, and Healm does not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

You can see, export, change, and delete your data in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your personal information. For anything you cannot do in the app, email hello@healm.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects what leaves your phone, Healm will tell you in the app before it applies.
