What Healm is not

  • Healm is not a medical device, a clinician, or an emergency service. No person reads your conversations or watches for emergencies.
  • An answer, a scan result, a label read, or a lab reading can be wrong or incomplete. AI models make mistakes. Check anything important with a clinician or pharmacist before you act on it.

  • Your daily number compares you with your own recent days. It is not a medical score.

Do not wait for Healm: if something feels serious, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
